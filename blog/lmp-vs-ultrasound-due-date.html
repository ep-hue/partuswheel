LMP vs Ultrasound Due Date: When Should the EDD Be Changed?

Which dating is more accurate, the ACOG Committee Opinion 700 redating thresholds by gestational age, and what to do with a 2-week difference or a third-trimester scan.

Published by 55DIGITS LLC · Last reviewed November 9, 2026 · Reference for healthcare professionals

Quick answer

Per ACOG Committee Opinion 700 (reaffirmed 2025), first-trimester ultrasound (up to 13w6d) is the most accurate dating method. Change the EDD to the ultrasound date when the LMP–ultrasound difference is more than 5 days before 9w0d, more than 7 days at 9w0d–15w6d, more than 10 days at 16w0d–21w6d, more than 14 days at 22w0d–27w6d and more than 21 days from 28w0d. Once set, later changes to the EDD are reserved for rare circumstances.

Which is more accurate: LMP or ultrasound?

Early ultrasound. LMP dating assumes a 28-day cycle with ovulation on day 14 and depends on recall; ACOG cites studies in which only about half of women recall their LMP accurately. Ultrasound measures the embryo or fetus directly, but its accuracy falls as pregnancy advances and individual size varies more:

Ultrasound gestational ageMethodAccuracy (ACOG CO 700)
Up to 13w6dCrown–rump length (CRL)±5–7 days
14w0d–21w6dComposite biometry±7–10 days
22w0d–27w6dComposite biometry±10–14 days
28w0d and laterComposite biometry±21–30 days

Beyond a CRL of 84 mm (about 14w0d), CRL loses accuracy and second-trimester biometry is used instead.

The ACOG redating thresholds

Gestational age by ultrasoundRedate if LMP and ultrasound differ by
Up to 8w6dmore than 5 days
9w0d – 13w6dmore than 7 days
14w0d – 15w6dmore than 7 days
16w0d – 21w6dmore than 10 days
22w0d – 27w6dmore than 14 days
28w0d and latermore than 21 days

Three details that are easy to get wrong:

  • The window is chosen by the ultrasound gestational age, not the LMP gestational age.
  • “More than” means strictly more. A difference exactly equal to the threshold keeps LMP dating.
  • Direction does not matter. The rule applies whether the ultrasound is ahead of or behind the LMP.

What if LMP and ultrasound are 2 weeks apart?

A 14-day difference means different things at different stages:

  • Ultrasound before 22w0d: 14 days exceeds the threshold (5, 7 or 10 days) → redate to ultrasound.
  • Ultrasound at 22w0d–27w6d: 14 days does not exceed 14 → keep LMP dating.
  • Ultrasound at 28w0d or later: below the 21-day threshold → keep LMP dating.

Can the due date change in the third trimester?

Rarely. Third-trimester ultrasound is the least reliable dating method (±21–30 days). ACOG supports adjusting the EDD when the first ultrasound of the pregnancy is done in the third trimester and the discrepancy is more than 21 days. A pregnancy without an ultrasound that confirms or revises the EDD before 22w0d is considered suboptimally dated (see ACOG Committee Opinion 688).

What about later scans?

Once the EDD has been set from the LMP, the first accurate ultrasound or both, ACOG recommends that it be discussed with the patient and documented clearly, and that subsequent changes be reserved for rare circumstances, so later scans do not routinely re-date the pregnancy. If the LMP is unknown, dating is based on ultrasound, with the earliest CRL measurement prioritized as the most reliable.

IVF pregnancies

If the pregnancy came from assisted reproduction, ACOG says the ART-derived gestational age — embryo age and transfer date — should be used to assign the EDD. See how to calculate a due date after IVF.

Common questions

When should the due date be changed based on ultrasound?

Per ACOG Committee Opinion 700, when the LMP–ultrasound difference is more than 5 days before 9w0d, more than 7 days at 9w0d–15w6d, more than 10 days at 16w0d–21w6d, more than 14 days at 22w0d–27w6d, or more than 21 days from 28w0d.

Is the ultrasound due date more accurate than the LMP due date?

First-trimester ultrasound (crown–rump length up to 13w6d) is the most accurate method, at about ±5–7 days. LMP dating depends on recall and a 28-day-cycle assumption.

What if my LMP and ultrasound are 2 weeks apart?

If the ultrasound was before 22w0d, a 14-day difference exceeds the ACOG threshold and the pregnancy is redated. At 22w0d–27w6d or later, 14 days does not exceed the threshold and LMP dating is kept.

Can the due date change after a later ultrasound?

Once the EDD is established, ACOG reserves subsequent changes for rare circumstances, so later scans do not routinely re-date the pregnancy.

What does suboptimally dated mean?

A pregnancy without an ultrasound examination that confirms or revises the EDD before 22 0/7 weeks of gestation (ACOG CO 700).
